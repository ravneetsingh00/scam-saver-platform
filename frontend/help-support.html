QUICK ANSWERS

Frequently asked questions

How do I submit a fraud report?

Open Submit a Report, complete the steps, review your information, then submit or keep it as a draft.

Where can I see my report status?

Open My Reports to view your submitted reports and their latest review status.

Can I edit my account information?

Open My Profile and choose Edit profile to update your name or email address.

Is my report automatically a finding of wrongdoing?

No. Reports are community signals and are reviewed; a submission does not establish wrongdoing.
