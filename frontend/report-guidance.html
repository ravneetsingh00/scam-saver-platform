Official reporting routes · India

Where Should I Report?

Choose the closest category to find the responsible authority and practical next steps.

Urgent financial cyber fraud: call 1930 immediately, contact your bank/payment provider, and file at the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal. The 1930 helpline is for cyber financial fraud; use the relevant regulator or consumer channel for other disputes.
UPI fraudBank or UPI app immediately; call 1930 and file on the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal.+

Report to: Bank or UPI app immediately; call 1930 and file on the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal.

Helpline: 1930

Open official reporting website ↗
  1. Call 1930 as soon as possible and report the transaction.
  2. Contact your bank and UPI app using their official app or number; ask them to block/freeze access and raise a dispute.
  3. Save UTR/RRN, transaction time, amount, screenshots and complaint acknowledgement.
  4. Complete the online cybercrime complaint and keep its acknowledgement number.
Banking fraudYour bank’s fraud line first; then 1930 and the cybercrime portal for cyber-enabled theft.+

Report to: Your bank’s fraud line first; then 1930 and the cybercrime portal for cyber-enabled theft.

Helpline: 1930

Open official reporting website ↗
  1. Call the bank using the number on your card or official website; block compromised cards or online access.
  2. Call 1930 promptly if money was transferred due to cyber fraud.
  3. File the cybercrime portal complaint and preserve bank SMS, statements and transaction IDs.
  4. Follow up with the bank in writing and retain its ticket number.
Online payment fraudPayment provider / bank, 1930 for cyber financial loss, and cybercrime portal.+

Report to: Payment provider / bank, 1930 for cyber financial loss, and cybercrime portal.

Helpline: 1930

Open official reporting website ↗
  1. Contact the payment provider and bank through official channels.
  2. Call 1930 immediately if funds were taken through online fraud.
  3. Submit the cybercrime portal complaint with transaction reference, merchant details and evidence.
  4. For a service or refund dispute without cyber theft, raise a merchant complaint and use National Consumer Helpline.
Fake investmentSEBI SCORES for grievances involving a listed company or SEBI-regulated entity; cybercrime portal for fraud/loss; Sachet for unauthorized deposits.+

Report to: SEBI SCORES for grievances involving a listed company or SEBI-regulated entity; cybercrime portal for fraud/loss; Sachet for unauthorized deposits.

Helpline: 1930 for cyber financial loss

Open official reporting website ↗
  1. Stop sending money and do not pay “recovery” fees.
  2. Verify whether the intermediary is registered on the official SEBI website.
  3. First complain to the regulated entity; if unresolved, lodge a grievance on SCORES.
  4. For an unauthorized deposit scheme, report it to RBI Sachet. If money was stolen online, call 1930 and file cybercrime report.
Job fraudCybercrime portal for online payment theft; National Consumer Helpline only for consumer service disputes.+

Report to: Cybercrime portal for online payment theft; National Consumer Helpline only for consumer service disputes.

Helpline: 1930 if money was lost online

Open official reporting website ↗
  1. Stop payments and do not share OTPs, bank credentials or identity documents with unverified recruiters.
  2. Save the job ad, recruiter profile, emails, chats and payment records.
  3. If money was taken through online fraud, call 1930 and file on the cybercrime portal.
  4. Report the fake account or listing on the platform where it appeared.
Social media fraudPlatform reporting tools; cybercrime portal for account takeover, impersonation, threats or money loss.+

Report to: Platform reporting tools; cybercrime portal for account takeover, impersonation, threats or money loss.

Helpline: 1930 for financial cyber fraud

Open official reporting website ↗
  1. Secure the account: change password from the official app/site, sign out other sessions and enable two-factor authentication.
  2. Report the profile, message or content to the platform.
  3. Preserve profile URLs, message IDs, dates and screenshots.
  4. If money was lost, call 1930 immediately and submit a cybercrime complaint.
Identity theftCybercrime portal; contact the affected bank, telecom provider or issuing authority.+

Report to: Cybercrime portal; contact the affected bank, telecom provider or issuing authority.

Helpline: 1930 if financial loss occurred

Open official reporting website ↗
  1. Secure affected accounts and call the bank/provider using official contact details.
  2. If a mobile number is being misused, check Sanchar Saathi and report suspicious communication via Chakshu.
  3. File a cybercrime report with copies of notices, account alerts and relevant IDs (redact unrelated sensitive data).
  4. Keep complaint references and monitor affected accounts.
Fake shopping websitesCybercrime portal for payment fraud; National Consumer Helpline for unresolved purchase/service grievances.+

Report to: Cybercrime portal for payment fraud; National Consumer Helpline for unresolved purchase/service grievances.

Helpline: 1930 if money was taken through cyber fraud · NCH 1915

Open official reporting website ↗
  1. Do not make further payments or install apps/APKs from the seller.
  2. Contact your bank/payment service to dispute or block the transaction.
  3. Call 1930 and report online payment fraud on the cybercrime portal.
  4. For ordinary product/service grievances, contact the seller first, then register with NCH at 1915 or online.
Suspicious callsSanchar Saathi Chakshu for suspected fraud communications; cybercrime portal if you became a victim.+

Report to: Sanchar Saathi Chakshu for suspected fraud communications; cybercrime portal if you became a victim.

Helpline: 1930 if money was lost

Open official reporting website ↗
  1. Do not share codes or follow remote-access instructions; hang up.
  2. Report the call/SMS/WhatsApp message through Chakshu on Sanchar Saathi.
  3. Keep the sender number, message, date/time and screenshots.
  4. If money or credentials were taken, contact the bank, call 1930 and file a cybercrime report.
Property fraudLocal police for suspected criminal fraud; cybercrime portal if it was conducted online; NCH for service disputes.+

Report to: Local police for suspected criminal fraud; cybercrime portal if it was conducted online; NCH for service disputes.

Helpline: 1930 for online financial loss

Open official reporting website ↗
  1. Stop further payments and preserve the agreement, receipts, listing, messages and identity details.
  2. Contact your bank/payment provider if a transfer was made.
  3. For online fraud/loss, call 1930 and report on the cybercrime portal; also contact local police for property-related criminal allegations.
  4. For a builder/service grievance, use the relevant state RERA or consumer commission/helpline as applicable.
Consumer complaintsNational Consumer Helpline, Department of Consumer Affairs.+

Report to: National Consumer Helpline, Department of Consumer Affairs.

Helpline: 1915 or 1800-11-4000

Open official reporting website ↗
  1. First contact the seller/service provider and keep the ticket or email.
  2. Collect invoice, order number, payment receipt, warranty and correspondence.
  3. Register through consumerhelpline.gov.in or call 1915 / 1800-11-4000.
  4. If the grievance is unresolved, check the official consumer commission process; cyber theft should also be reported via 1930.

This is general guidance, not legal advice. Verify current instructions on the linked official portal.
