PRACTICAL SAFETY GUIDES

A few checks for common fraud situations

Dealer, vehicle and repair fraud

  • Verify seller identity, vehicle registration and chassis/VIN against documents; get an independent inspection and written invoice.
  • Confirm the final price, loan terms, warranty and add-ons in writing before signing or transferring money.
  • Pay the named business through traceable channels; be cautious if payment is redirected to an unrelated personal account.

Property, rental and builder offers

  • See the property in person and independently verify ownership or the agent’s authority with the relevant records and local registration/RERA resources.
  • Read the agreement, refund terms, possession date and fee breakdown before paying a token or deposit.
  • Save the listing, receipts, messages and signed documents; avoid pressure to pay before checks are complete.

Job, work-from-home and recruitment scams

  • Confirm the role on the employer’s own careers website and contact the company using independently found details.
  • Pause if asked for interview, registration, training, equipment or security fees, especially to a personal account.
  • Never send an OTP, UPI PIN, password or remote-access permission to a recruiter.

Shopping, delivery and consumer disputes

  • Check the exact store domain, seller history, return policy and total price before checkout.
  • Do not scan a QR code or enter a UPI PIN to receive a refund. Contact the merchant and payment provider through their official channels.
  • For a consumer service dispute, retain the invoice and complaint number and use the National Consumer Helpline if needed.

Banking, UPI, investment and loan fraud

  • Never share an OTP, UPI PIN or banking password; call your bank using the number on its official app/card/site.
  • Check investment intermediaries and lenders with the relevant regulator. Guaranteed returns, urgency and “recovery” fees are warning signs.
  • If cyber financial money is taken, contact the bank and call 1930 promptly; keep transaction references and report through the official portal.

Identity, social media and impersonation

  • Verify requests for money or documents with the person or organization through a separate, trusted channel.
  • Secure compromised accounts from the official app, change passwords, revoke unknown sessions and enable multi-factor authentication.
  • Save profile links and messages, report impersonation to the platform and contact the relevant authority if identity documents are misused.
